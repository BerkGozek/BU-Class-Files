## Writing Style Reference

- `Writing Examples/` contains the user's past writing from **other, unrelated courses/contexts** — the content has nothing to do with CASAR 280S.
- Whenever drafting any new writing for this course (essays, journals, analyses, reflections), automatically read these PDFs and match the user's voice, tone, structure, and sentence-level habits — no need to ask permission first. Do not pull content or claims from them, only style.
- Otherwise, do NOT reference, open, or bring up files from this folder in conversation unless the user explicitly asks about them. In particular, do not assume a question about "the assignment" or "the journal" refers to something in this folder — check `Assignments/` and `Modules/*/Work Done/` for actual CASAR 280S coursework first.

## Writing Requirements (Essays/Journals)

Before finalizing any essay or journal for this course, check:

- Follow required formatting
- Include all required assignment components (e.g., Works Cited, recipe, appendix, illustrations/images, etc.)
- When in-text citation and a Works Cited section are required, use **Chicago author-date** citation style
- Include scientific names of plants/animals at their first mention, e.g. maize (*Zea mays*), sheep (*Ovis aries*)
- Proofread for grammar, spelling, and clarity
- If facilitator feedback exists on a prior essay in the series (e.g., Food Journal Essay #1), review it and avoid repeating the same mistakes in later essays
