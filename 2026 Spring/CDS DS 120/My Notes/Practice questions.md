# Mathematical Statements
1. Negate the following statements
	1. $\forall x\in \mathbb{R}.x<0\vee x>0$
	2. $\exists x\in \mathbb{N}. x<10\wedge x>5$
	3. $\exists x \in \mathbb{R}.\forall y\in \mathbb{R}. x>y \rightarrow x^2>y^2$
	4. $\forall x\in \mathbb{R}. y=x^3$
	5. $\forall x \in \mathbb{R}.neg P(x), \text{where is P(x) given by: }\forall y\in \mathbb{R}. x=y^3$
	6. $\forall a \in A. \exists b\in C. \exists d,e\in D. P(a,b,c,d,e)$
2. Determine the truth values of the following mathematical statements:
	1. $(1+2=3)\rightarrow (\forall x \in \mathbb{R}.x^2\geq0)$
	2. $(1+2=5)\rightarrow(\forall x\in \mathbb{R}.x^2\geq 0)$
	3. $(1+3=5)\rightarrow(\text{Donald Trump is our current president.})$
	4. $(1+6=7)\rightarrow(0=2)$
	5. $(Pythagorean\ Theorem)\rightarrow(2=2)$
	6. $(0=1)\rightarrow(3=7)$
	7. $(2+3=5)\vee(\forall x\in \mathbb{R}.x^2>0)$
	8. $(1+4=3)\vee(\forall x \in \mathbb{R}.x^2\geq0)$
	9. $(1+3=5)\vee(\exists x\in \mathbb{R}.x^2<0)$
3. Let S and T be arbitrary sets. Let P be "$S\subseteq T$" and let Q be "$S-T=\emptyset$"
   Find the truth value of:
	1. $P\rightarrow Q$
	2. $Q\rightarrow P$
	3. $\neg Q \rightarrow \neg P$
	4. $\neg P \rightarrow \neg Q$
