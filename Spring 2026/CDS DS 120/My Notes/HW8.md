# 1 - From 8 men and 6 women, form a committee of 5 such that at least 2 women are included. **5 points

4 combinations:
- 0M+5W
- 1M+4W
- 2M+3W
- 3M+2W

$$
\begin{aligned}
0M+5W = \binom{8}{0}\binom{6}{5} = 1 *6  = 6  \\
1M+4W = \binom{8}{1}\binom{6}{4} = 8 *15 = 120\\
2M+3W = \binom{8}{2}\binom{6}{3} = 28*20 = 560 \\
3M+2W = \binom{8}{3}\binom{6}{2} = 56*15 = 840\\
6+120+560+840 = 1526
\end{aligned}
$$
