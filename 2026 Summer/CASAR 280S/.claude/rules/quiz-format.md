# Quiz Format — Short Essay / Open-Ended Questions

Per `Lecture Slides/` (source: Week 2 deck, "Format for Quiz 1" / "Short Essay" slides). Each module quiz has 15 short questions (MC/matching/ordering/fill-in-blank, 1 pt each) plus **1 randomized short essay question worth 10 pts**, in the format: *"You are an archaeologist studying ___ in ___ region."* The scenario gives a specific food/topic and region, then asks some combination of:

- How would you excavate the site? Why?
- What methods would you use? Why?
- What analyses would you perform? Why?
- How would you interpret the data you get? What is your reasoning? What conclusions can you make?

When drafting an answer to this type of question:

- Answer in **complete sentences, no bullet points**
- **Fully explain the logic/reasoning** behind each answer, not just the answer itself
- **Answer every component** of the question — don't skip any sub-part
- Draw on the module's actual methods/key terms (e.g. survey, LiDAR, geophysics, excavation, stratigraphy, flotation, paleoethnobotany, chemical residue analysis, zooarchaeology, radiocarbon dating) rather than generic descriptions

This format may recur across future quizzes covering later modules — check the relevant week's `Lecture Slides/` deck for confirmation/updates before assuming it's identical.
